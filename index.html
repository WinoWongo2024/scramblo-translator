Repeated-letter compression

PairCompressed

Compression runs after scrambling. Three identical letters → special + remaining letter (e.g. eee → êe). Special characters expand back before reverse scrambling.

Capitalization: only the first character of the final word is uppercase. Questions receive an inverted ¿ at the start. Punctuation, numbers, emojis and hyphens are preserved.
