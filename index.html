Language Rules

Scrambling permutations

LengthPositions (1-based)

1–2 letter words unchanged. Lengths 3–10 use fixed rules. Lengths 11+ use: last letter first, then remaining even positions, then odd positions.

Repeated-letter compression

PairCompressed

Compression after scrambling. Three identical letters → special + remaining letter (e.g. eee → êe).

Only the first character of the final word is uppercase. Questions get a leading ¿. Punctuation, numbers, emojis preserved.
